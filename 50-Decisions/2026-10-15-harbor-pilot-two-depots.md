# Pilot route changes at 2 depots before full rollout

## Decision
Run the new routing at Eastside and Westside depots for 6 weeks before rolling out to all 6.

## Why
Dana didn't want to change all drivers' routes at once before peak season. Two depots give enough data to compare against the other four.

## Options considered
- Full rollout to all 6 depots — rejected, too risky before peak season
- One depot only — rejected, not enough data

## What changes
Results due 2026-11-14. Full rollout decision after that.
