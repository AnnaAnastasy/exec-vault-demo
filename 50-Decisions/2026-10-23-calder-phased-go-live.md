# Propose phased go-live for Calder ERP

## Decision
Split the Calder ERP go-live into two phases: finance + orders on 2027-01-15 as planned, inventory module after the vendor delivers the export (estimated early January).

## Why
The warehouse system vendor says the inventory data export may not be ready until early January, putting the full 2027-01-15 go-live at risk of slipping 2-3 weeks. A phased approach keeps the original date for modules that are ready.

## Options considered
- Full go-live on Jan 15 as planned (risk: inventory data not ready)
- Delay entire go-live by 2-3 weeks (Tom unhappy with this)
- Phased go-live: finance+orders Jan 15, inventory after (Maria's suggestion)
